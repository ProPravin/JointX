Joint Care Guidance

Recommended

Low-impact physical activity. Walking, swimming or cycling to keep joints mobile without added strain. Joint mobility exercises. Gentle daily stretches to maintain range of motion. Healthy weight management. Reducing body weight lowers load transmitted through the knee joint. Balanced nutrition. Anti-inflammatory foods can support joint health over time. Avoid prolonged joint loading. Limit long periods of squatting, kneeling or standing still.
